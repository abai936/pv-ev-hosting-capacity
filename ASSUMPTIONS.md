## Stage 9b: uptake scenarios (modelled forecast, national data)

| # | Assumption | Where | Effect | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A30 | EV measure = BEV per residential ICP = BEV stock (MoT via Figure.NZ, all vehicle classes) / the monthly residential ICP count of the same month from the EA file (data; the EA file runs Aug 2013 to Oct 2026, so it covers every BEV month Jan 2016 to Feb 2026 and nothing is filled). Persons per household no longer drives it (column renamed from ev_share_of_households_pct to bev_per_res_icp_pct; the +/-15 % persons-per-household sensitivity is dropped). Headline 6 therefore compares an upper bound with the Stage 7 'EVs of 55'. 'Per 1,000 people' uses ICPs per person (EA ICPs / ERP at Feb 2026, ERP extrapolated) | `uptake.py`, `stage9b_uptake.py` | EV reading against the Stage 7 counts | checked (arithmetic from two saved files); that it bounds the share of houses with an EV is believed |
| A31 | Logistic ceilings are scenario parameters, not fitted: PV 15/30/60 % of houses, EV 20/40/70 % of households. No source found | `uptake.py` | shape of the S-curve; best back-tested model was naive, so mid scenarios do not depend on them | guessed |
| A32 | Driver release lags (CPI 1 month, population 3, MBIE price 6 months) and a blanking rule: price growth blank after Mar 2026, population growth blank after Sep 2026. A32 addendum (Stage 9b review, 19 Nov 2026), forecast honesty notes (believed, not checked):
- Two small leakages in the first version: the EV share used population interpolated toward the next 30 June ERP (released after the origin), and a constant persons-per-household from Jun 2025 was applied at all origins; each about 0.01 pp or less. Both were removed from the EV share by the A30 change (denominator = same-month EA ICP count). What remains: the same-month ICP count is treated as known at the origin although it is published with a delay (believed small), and 'per 1,000 people' still uses interpolated ERP but is not used in any forecast.
- The ceilings (A31), model windows (36/60 months, grids), the three origins (Dec 2021 to Dec 2023) and the rule 'kept = within 2x of best MAE' were chosen in Nov 2026 with all data already visible, so the back-test is not a clean out-of-sample test.
