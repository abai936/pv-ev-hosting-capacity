# Limitations (Stage 9, 19 Nov 2026)

Plain list of what this project cannot tell you. Everything is **simulated**. Bias direction: **HC↑** means hosting capacity (HC) looks bigger than reality, **HC↓** smaller. The entries (A1 to A29) are in `ASSUMPTIONS.md`. Evidence for each check is in `VALIDATION.md`.

1. **Simulation only.** No hardware, no measured feeder data. Nothing is compared with a real network. Bias: unknown.
2. **Synthetic data.** The load and EV shapes are synthetic and NZ-parameterised (Steidl et al. 2025); PV is modelled (PVGIS-ERA5). The EV session median (6.9 kWh) is from a search snippet only. Bias: HC↑ for PV (A16: no shading, soiling or local weather), HC↓ for EV if real evening charging is tighter (A18).
3. **Line ratings are assumed (A5).** The feeder file has no current ratings, so ratings came from cited Australian datasheets matched by resistance. Two codes (`2c_.007`, `4c_.35`) are low confidence and probably too high (HC↑). Effect on results: the thermal limit, not voltage, binds in almost every case. The 60% PV headline of 5.0 kW is thermal; voltage alone would allow 6.0 kW (CP-06). The EV count is a step function of the assumed trunk rating: a 5% change moved it from 6 to 9 (CP-07). Read thermal results as relative, not absolute. Even with no PV or EV the assumed trunk is exceeded about 1.5% of the year (CP-06).
4. **30-minute steps hide peaks (A21).** Short peaks are averaged away. Bias: HC↑ and violation hours too low.
5. **Voltage unbalance limit is 2% (A7).** It is the European (EN 50160) basis and we found no NZ limit. VUF never bound in the Stage 6 snapshot (max 1.07% at the HC points, `stage6_snapshot_summary.md`; the 1.18% was Stage 4 L1). The no-PV year runs already exceed 2% VUF: Y0 2.32% for 6 h, and 2.3 to 2.9% across the 5 seeds, so VUF is not clean in the year runs. Bias: unknown.
6. **One feeder (A26).** The IEEE European LV feeder with 55 customers and an 800 kVA transformer. Bias: unknown for any other network.
7. **Neutral and earthing are simplified.** The neutral is Kron-reduced (A12, HC↑: neutral shift is not seen). A neutral-return correction is applied to the 2c and 35_SAC service cables (A4, about 0.5 V). It is a correction toward physics, not a bias. There is no earthing, no neutral-voltage output and no harmonic study.
8. **The snapshot method is Orion-style, not a standard (A22).** It uses median midday summer load (PV) or median winter evening load (EV). Zero load gave a lower HC, and a busy evening gave 2 EVs instead of 6. Bias: HC↑ versus a minimum-load or design-peak method.
9. **House diversity (A15, A20).** Each house picks one of the feeder's own 100 daily profiles (probably UK) per day, and all houses are scaled to a mean of 7,048 kWh. Every house is statistically identical over a year, so heavy-user and light-user homes are missing. Missing a light-load worst-case house makes the modelled voltage rise smaller, so for voltage-limited PV and for EV the bias is HC↑ (optimistic). The 5 diversity seeds only reshuffle days among statistically identical houses, so they cannot test house-to-house differences; the seed results (CP-06) are not evidence against this bias.
10. **PV and load timing offsets (A25).** PV is at the step start, load is the step mean, and the daylight-saving handling differs by 1 h in summer between sources. Bias: unknown, small.
11. **Var-priority is an assumption (A24).** OpenDSS cuts real power first to make room for reactive power. No source says Australia A requires this. Bias is MIXED (`stage8_summary.md`): HC↑ where voltage binds (P2 30% tap 1.0: M3 6.1 kW vs M0 5.4 kW), slightly HC↓ where the assumed thermal limit binds (P1 30% tap 1.0: M3 9.3 vs M0 9.5 kW), curtailment↑. With inverter kVA = 1.1 x kW the HC was lower than kVA = kW in all 24 M2/M3 cases (0.02 to 0.65 kW), so the size setting matters. The inverter curves come from free summaries, not the paywalled standard (A8).
12. **Source voltage is a setting (A1).** 1.00 pu is the base case; 1.05 pu cuts the 60% result from 5.0 to 0.9 kW. A real feeder's voltage depends on the upstream network, which is not modelled. Bias: strong, either direction.
13. **Transformer rating (A6).** 800 kVA nameplate with no cyclic rating. The transformer never bound (max 140 to 217 kVA). Bias: HC↓ if it ever bound.
14. **Validation is mostly bookkeeping beyond the benchmark.** The energy balances only prove signs and units. The two hand checks of voltage rise share the path resistance with the model. Only the published-solution benchmark (3 mV) and the toy circuits are independent checks of the network (see `VALIDATION.md`).
15. **Licence.** The IEEE feeder licence is not stated, so no feeder file is committed. Run `scripts/download_data.py` to fetch it (A27).
16. **No battery, no tariffs, no costs, no multi-feeder or MV model.** Out of scope by the plan.
